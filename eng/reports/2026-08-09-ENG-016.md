Estimated CTA position on iPhone SE (375×667): ~290px from top. Fold is at 667px.

## Why it was below the fold

The `@media (max-width:820px)` rule adds `.cta-preview { display:block }` (the
decorative SMS preview bubble). In the single-column mobile layout, the DOM
order is: eyebrow → h1 → hero-copy → cta-preview → CTA button. The hero-copy
(~100px) plus cta-preview (~74px) together pushed the button below 667px even
after the prior padding-top reduction (ENG-014 comment said "CTA clears the fold"
but the 820px cta-preview was added afterward and broke that assumption).
