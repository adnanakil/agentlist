### 2. Why 0 real taps (the core question)

The 0-tap result is NOT a broken link mechanic. Two factors explain it:

**Factor A — Real paid traffic is tiny:**
- utm_source=google: 23 total views (17 mobile) in 14 days
- utm_source=ig: 80 total views (41 in-app browser + 39 external) in 14 days
- Total real paid mobile users seeing the sms: CTA: ~58
- With 58 people, 0 taps is consistent with conversion rates below 1.7%

**Factor B — Traffic stats were heavily bot-inflated:**
95.8% of recorded "non-bot" hits had no UTM source. Investigation revealed:
- `facebookexternalhit/1.1` (Facebook link-preview crawler): **624 hits** not caught as bots
- `Version/17.0 Mobile Safari Verify/NNN` scanner bots: **~200+ hits**
- iOS 10–14 spoofed UA bots (e.g. `iPhone; CPU iPhone OS 13_2_3`): **544 hits**
- `CMS-Checker/1.0`, `SiteInspector/1.0`, `GoFastSiteChecker`: smaller numbers
- `GoogleOther`: uncounted

The apparent "879 iOS/iPhone Safari" hits are mostly bots — real human iOS paid-traffic is far smaller.
